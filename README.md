## Proof of work

**Scholars Hunt — competition platform** · `Laravel 12` `MySQL`

Registration and payment platform for an English competition in Sylhet — 6,000 participants from 150+ institutes. Online + offline registration, manual bKash and cash payment tracking, auto-generated admit cards, and an admin panel a non-technical team runs daily. Around 1,100 automated tests.

Live: [englishscholarshunt.com](https://englishscholarshunt.com)

**Addopanto Flow** · `React` `TypeScript` `Supabase`

Multi-tenant financial management for schools and coaching centers — fee collection, payroll, expense tracking. In active development.
